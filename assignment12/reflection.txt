Static visualizations are fixed and straightforward, 
like a simple chart in a report. They’re good for quick communication 
but you can’t do much beyond looking at them. Interactive visualizations, 
on the other hand, let you explore—filter, zoom, and adjust things. 
That makes it easier to catch patterns you might miss in a static view.

Dashboards are useful because they put everything in one place. Instead of 
creating new charts every time, you can check real-time updates, compare 
different pieces of data, and get answers right away. It saves time and 
makes it easier to actually see what’s going on.

Tools like Plotly and Dash make sharing data more effective. Interactive 
plots help people engage with the information instead of just staring 
at a flat graph. It makes communication clearer, helps others understand 
faster, and feels more professional in a team or workplace setting.
